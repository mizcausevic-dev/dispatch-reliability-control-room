# Origin

Dispatch reliability is not just a routing problem. It is a handoff, readiness, and SLA problem. The moment a route slips, teams need a surface that maps queue pressure to blockers, route drift, and the next safe move.

We built `dispatch-reliability-control-room` to make that operating layer explicit. The point is not to replace a TMS or field-dispatch product. The point is to show what a Mobility / Transportation operator surface should look like when the audience needs to manage route pressure, partner handoffs, and SLA posture with real customer consequences attached.
